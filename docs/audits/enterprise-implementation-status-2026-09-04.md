Connection changes clear transient results so one workspace's decision is not silently saved into another. Historical saved results validate against their own frozen locations rather than the current mutable registry.

Failed reloads preserve the valid connection; edits are blocked during a connection read.

- Evidence review retains artifacts, actor/reason/time, accepted publications, and soft-retired history. Refresh adapters exhaust pagination and quarantine candidates instead of overwriting accepted bundles. The mounted workbench exposes real review decisions and publication lineage.

A real browser test confirms the 38 → 37 review count and the recorded decision survive reload/reconnection.
- Modeled, public-facility, and draft markers now preserve MapLibre's absolute positioning; an overriding relative-position rule had displaced markers by render order. Nearby facilities open a scoped overlap chooser with every peer reachable, keyboard selection, and Escape focus restoration.
- The assistant is question-sensitive and grounded in bounded deterministic evidence/model tools. It openly states that no language-model provider is connected and cannot modify the model.

Use a common collision layout that reserves marker bounds, prioritizes selected/critical labels, and reveals the rest on zoom or through the synchronized facility list.

- **Decision-first comparison.** Bring period selection and product scope closer to the map, make current selection unmistakable, and give each result a concise causal explanation and drill-through to the limiting product, facility, lane, and assumption. Any time scrubber must show period-effective topology, not imply the final-day map represents the whole horizon.
- **Graceful renderer failure.** Bundled geography avoids external tile dependence, but a WebGL failure still needs a local map fallback with an accessible facility/impact list, without taking down the rest of the workspace.
- **Evidence comprehension.** Keep the source date, precision, lifecycle, and unknown operating facts visible. A facility-count badge or green identity symbol must never imply a complete, currently operating supply network or verified allocation territories.

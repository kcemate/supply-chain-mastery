# Flyway complete UI/UX audit — 2026-08-23

## Verdict

Pass for the requested single-user prototype and responsive desktop/tablet experience. All Critical and High interaction findings discovered in the baseline were resolved, the repaired core flows were re-walked, eight representative views pass automated WCAG A/AA rules, and the final public build has no observed console, page, first-party request, or first-party response errors.

## Scope and method

- Persona: U.S. restaurant supply-chain executive with high domain knowledge, moderate patience, desktop/tablet use, and a need to understand network posture within 30 seconds.
- Baseline: `https://awake-timber-px5z.here.now/` before the improvement pass.
- Final: `https://verdant-charm-5zhz.here.now/`.
- Interaction evidence: three baseline walkthrough attempts totaling more than five minutes, with a 40-action completed manifest covering onboarding, Control Tower, evidence atlas, facility authoring, connections, scenarios, Copilot, sources, settings, persistence, keyboard use, responsive layouts, reduced motion, and browser health.
- Visual evidence: 1,920, 1,600, 1,440, 1,024, 768, and 375 pixel viewport checks; desktop, tablet, mobile, drawers, review queue, dark theme, and live-host screenshots were inspected.
- Accessibility: keyboard interaction, focus restoration, accessible-name inventory, 44×44 target sweep, reduced-motion check, and axe WCAG 2 A/AA + 2.1 A/AA scans on eight representative views.
- Product verification: deterministic engine, public evidence integrity, production build, desktop/tablet browser workflow, design-system lint, whitespace validation, live SPA fallback, and live browser health.

The completed interaction manifest and raw findings are in `tasks/ui-ux-audit-2026-08-23/manifest/`. After evidence is in `tasks/ui-ux-audit-2026-08-23/after/`.

| Severity | Finding | Disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Five most important improvements

1. **Trustworthy controls:** every visible top-level action now has an outcome; false filters, columns, map settings, and context affordances are gone or functional.
2. **Honest evidence review:** freshness review exposes all 38 unresolved evidence records instead of presenting a false conflict-free state.
3. **Complete executive workflows:** facilities can be created, edited, connected, and persisted; scenarios can be rerun, saved, and branched from recommendations with explicit feedback.
4. **Keyboard, touch, and accessibility:** semantic rows, Escape dismissal, trigger focus restoration, labels, 44px targets, responsive navigation, reduced motion, and WCAG AA contrast all pass.
5. **Purposeful motion and polish:** anchored dropdowns, synchronized drawers/scrims, modal entry, validation shake, tab feedback, and transient toasts use shared motion tokens and disable cleanly for reduced-motion users.
